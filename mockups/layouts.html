iSmith layout mockups

Space rail on the left, the selected space’s tabs across the top. Click a space in the rail to swap in its tabs. Click a group label in the tab strip to collapse or expand it. Click + in the rail to try setting up a new client space. Outlook, DevOps and Teams are ordinary tabs; the rail’s unread counts come from whatever tabs are open in each space. All mail and tickets are example data.

Each space uses one account per provider. Contoso and Fabrikam both show Outlook at the same URL, and the chip shows which Microsoft account is in use. The three dock buttons in the toolbar are the browser’s own setting for the agent panel.
